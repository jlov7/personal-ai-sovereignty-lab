| Workflow | Evidence Role |
| --- | --- |

The local umbrella gate is `pnpm verify`, which runs deterministic eval generation, artifact manifest regeneration and verification, the Vitest suite, and the production build.

## Review Policy

If a public issue, external annotation, baseline submission, aggregate attack, or scorecard dispute contradicts a claim in this index, the claim should be downgraded before the project is promoted. Preserving negative evidence is part of the benchmark standard. The phrase independent validation must be reserved for evidence produced by reviewers or systems outside the author-maintainer loop.
